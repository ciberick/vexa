- **Opt-in screen + audio recording for Google Meet (video).** `POST /bots` accepts `video_recording: {"enabled": true, "fps": 5}` (5 or 10 fps; Google Meet only, requires `recording_enabled`; anything else is a 422). The bot streams the Xvfb display and the meeting audio as one WebM, uploaded in ~10 s chunks, so a crash keeps everything already uploaded. Tuned for slides and desktop sharing (~0.6 Mb/s target). A repeated chunk upload is a no-op and the same chunk with different bytes is a 409; a take with audio and video completes only when both have finished. Without the opt-in nothing changes.
